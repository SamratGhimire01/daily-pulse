# Tech Update

## Headline

Microsoft to hold Windows and Surface event in San Francisco focusing on local AI

## Summary

Microsoft is hosting an event in San Francisco to discuss what is next for Windows and Surface, specifically focusing on how local AI will shape the next chapter of the PC. The conversation will feature Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, and Windows and Surface chief Pavan Davuluri. 

## Why It Matters

Developers working on desktop platforms will want to watch how local AI integration and hardware partnerships between Microsoft and Nvidia evolve. These shifts directly impact client-side AI capabilities and performance expectations for modern PCs.
