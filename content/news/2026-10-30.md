# Tech Update

## Headline

Hands-On Impressions of the Touchscreen MacBook Pro

## Summary

Apple has released its long-awaited touchscreen MacBook Pro, officially named the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display. Hands-on testing reveals that despite defying past expectations from Apple CEOs, the device feels surprisingly normal to use and features an exceptionally nice screen.

## Why It matters

It marks a major shift in Apple's hardware design philosophy for professional laptops, integrating touch interaction into a high-end macOS environment.
