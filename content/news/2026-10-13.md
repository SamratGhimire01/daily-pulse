# Tech Update

## Headline

Nvidia's DLSS 5 Receives Adjustments Ahead of Hogwarts Legacy Release

## Summary

Nvidia is toning down DLSS 5 for its upcoming debut in Hogwarts Legacy next month after initial demonstrations raised concerns. When first shown, the generative AI video game technology drew criticism for drastically transforming character faces, such as making a 15-year-old student look much older.

## Why It Matters

Developers working with graphics rendering and AI-driven upscaling technologies need to carefully balance visual fidelity and character preservation to avoid uncanny or unintended modifications.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Discovery Plus Increases Prices for Ad-Supported and Ad-Free Plans

## Summary

Discovery Plus is raising the monthly prices for both its ad-supported and ad-free streaming tiers, with the ad-supported plan moving from $5.99 to $6.99 and the ad-free tier increasing from $9.99 to $11.99. This price hike occurs just one week after Paramount completed its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery.

## Why It Matters

Changes in streaming subscription tiers and corporate acquisitions can impact platform integrations, APIs, and business models for engineers working in media and subscription-based tech ecosystems.

## Source

The Verge - https://www.theverge.com/streaming/1009994/discovery-plus-price-increase
