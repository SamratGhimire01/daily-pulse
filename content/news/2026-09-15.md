# Tech Update

## Headline

GM updates truck native software and adds picture-in-picture CarPlay support

## Summary

General Motors is updating the software in its bestselling trucks to reduce the amount of tapping and swiping needed for common controls. The update also introduces new picture-in-picture displays for Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, shifting from the company's previously stated hostility toward phone mirroring.

## Why It Matters

Developers working on automotive interfaces must balance native software controls with third-party phone mirroring systems like CarPlay and Android Auto. This shift highlights a design move toward reducing driver distraction through better UI layout and picture-in-picture integration.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Postpandemic tech bust and layoffs drive tech billionaires toward Donald Trump

## Summary

Following mass layoffs in 2022 and 2023, many tech workers turned against their employers. When liberal politicians subsequently rallied against Silicon Valley, the industry's backers shifted their support to Donald Trump.

## Why It Matters

Macroeconomic shifts, layoffs, and political friction can rapidly alter the relationship between industry backers, tech leadership, and government policy. Developers tracking the broader tech landscape should note how economic instability in the sector intersects with political realignments.

Surveillance concerns rise as dates report being covertly filmed with Meta smart glasses

## Summary

As anxieties regarding surveillance technology grow, singles are sharing experiences of being covertly filmed on dates by men wearing Meta smart glasses. Many individuals have labeled this behavior an instant red flag.

The rise of consumer surveillance hardware creates critical privacy and ethical challenges for developers and hardware designers. Building recording devices with subtle form factors requires careful consideration of consent and social boundaries to prevent user exploitation.
