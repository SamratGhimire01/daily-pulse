# Tech Update

## Headline

Apple uses software workaround to keep iPhone 18 Pro Max within battery shipping limits

## Summary

Apple has implemented a battery-firmware solution on the new iPhone 18 Pro Max to keep its capacity below the 20Wh single-cell battery limit set by international shipping regulations. This software workaround helps the device avoid shipping restrictions on big batteries.

## Why It Matters

It highlights how hardware design limitations can sometimes be successfully navigated through clever firmware and software controls during the logistics and supply chain process.

# Tech Update

## Headline

iFixit teardown reveals the iPhone 18 Pro's mechanical camera mechanism

## Summary

iFixit's teardown of the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max details the inner workings of their new variable aperture lens. The setup includes an adjustable mechanical iris with six leaf-like overlapping blades that open and close magnetically to control light, alongside some associated repair considerations.

## Why It matters

Combining intricate mechanical actuators like magnetic variable apertures into consumer smartphones introduces complex hardware design challenges and unique repairability considerations for hardware engineers.
