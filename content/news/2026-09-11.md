## Headline

Insta360 Releases the Luna Pro Single-Lens Steadicam Gimbal

## Summary

Following its two-lens Luna Ultra, Insta360 has launched a stripped-back Pro model featuring a single lens. This new gimbal offers the unique advantage of shooting vertical video in 4K and is available to buy in the US.

## Why It Matters

Hardware developers and tech-minded content creators tracking video gear will note its capability to natively capture 4K vertical video. It also presents a readily available US alternative to DJI's Osmo Pocket 4.
