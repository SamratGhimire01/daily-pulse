# Tech Update

## Headline

Spooky new infostealer malware uses AI to autodox victims

## Summary

A newly uncovered malware named “Volta-Autodoxer” leverages artificial intelligence tools to gather large amounts of victim data. The stolen information ranges from login credentials and credit card numbers to Discord sessions and UberEats receipts.

## Why It Matters

Security-conscious developers must be aware of evolving infostealer threats that integrate AI to automate data harvesting and credential theft.
