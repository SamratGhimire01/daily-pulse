# Tech Update

## Headline

Netflix Shifts Strategy from AAA Game Streaming to Marketing Major Titles

## Summary

Netflix has abandoned its plans to host AAA games directly on its streaming platform. Instead, the company is shifting its focus toward promoting highly anticipated external titles, such as Grand Theft Auto VI.

## Why It Matters

This pivot highlights the high technical and strategic hurdles of pushing into cloud or platform-native gaming, forcing even major tech companies to reassess their infrastructure goals.

## Headline

Cloudflare Optimizes 1.1.1.1 DNS Cache to Save 100 Terabytes of Memory

## Summary

Cloudflare has successfully implemented memory optimizations for the DNS cache of its 1.1.1.1 service. This architectural update resulted in a massive savings of 100 terabytes of memory.

## Why It Matters

Low-level cache optimization and memory management at internet scale offer valuable insights for developers looking to reduce resource overhead in high-throughput systems.
