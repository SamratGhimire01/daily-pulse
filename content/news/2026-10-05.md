# Tech Update

## Headline

OpenAI Introduces Visual Ads to ChatGPT Image Generation

## Summary

OpenAI is expanding advertisements in ChatGPT by introducing a new format that displays images of sponsored products and services on the screen. Testing for these visual ads will begin in the US later this month, initially appearing when users generate images, following OpenAI's initial introduction of ads to ChatGPT in February.

## Why It matters

Developers integrating or interacting with generative AI platforms need to monitor how monetization strategies and sponsored content formats evolve within major AI user interfaces.
