## Headline

Google Announces Fitbit Edge Wearable

## Summary

Google has introduced the Fitbit Edge, a new wearable priced at $179.99. It combines a sleek feel similar to the Fitbit Air with an OLED display, serving as a middle ground between the Air and the Pixel Watch.

## Why It Matters

Developers working in hardware ecosystems or health tech may want to note Google's ongoing adjustments to its wearable product tiers and display choices.
