# Tech Update

## Headline

Hackers Access Flock Camera Data and System Logs

## Summary

A hacker collective disassembled a Flock camera and dumped its data, revealing thousands of videos and system logs. The compromised files showed that a single device captured 1.6 million images of 50,000 vehicles over a 21-day period.

## Why It Matters

This data leak exposes real-world telemetry volumes and operational data structures of widespread automated surveillance hardware, highlighting significant IoT privacy and security risks.

## Source

WIRED (https://www.wired.com/story/hackers-flock-camera-data-shows-how-system-works/)
