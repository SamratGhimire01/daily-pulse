Democrats to Take Control of Congressional Committees, Signaling Increased Scrutiny for Tech

## Summary

Following a historic win of majorities in both the House and Senate, Democrats are set to take control of committee gavels in January. This shift is expected to bring tech CEO hearings, tougher AI and data center guardrails, and investigations into Elon Musk's DOGE cuts. The top Democrat on the House Oversight Committee responded with the warning to "LAWYER UP."

## Why It Matters

Developers and tech companies should anticipate a heavily regulated landscape with increased federal scrutiny, congressional hearings, and stricter compliance requirements for AI and data infrastructure.

NASA Announces the X-70 Fractal as its Newest Experimental X-Plane

## Summary

NASA has announced the X-70 Fractal, adding to its historic line of experimental X-series aircraft that includes the sound-barrier-breaking Bell X-1 and the X-59 quiet supersonic demonstrator. The new aircraft is built to serve as a high-performance and highly adaptable research platform.

## Why It Matters

It highlights ongoing aerospace innovation and new high-performance research platforms that push the boundaries of aviation engineering and experimental design.
