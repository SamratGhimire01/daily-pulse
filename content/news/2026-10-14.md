Tesla Roadster expected to make production-ready debut after nearly a decade

## Summary

It has been almost ten years since Elon Musk initially made a surprise announcement regarding plans to build a next-generation Tesla Roadster. The vehicle, which was supposed to be one of the fastest ever made, is now finally expected to make its production-ready debut.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Fairphone releases the Fairbuds 2 with improved audio, battery life, and repairability

## Summary

Fairphone has released its second pair of earbuds, the Fairbuds 2, which maintain the repairability of the first generation while delivering improved audio and almost twice the battery life. They are available now in the UK and Europe starting at £99 / €119, with a US launch priced at $119.

## Why It Matters

For developers interested in sustainable hardware design and modular electronics, Fairphone's continued push into repairable consumer audio provides a reference point for maintainable product lifecycles.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Scimplify raises $25M and scales revenue as it uses AI to challenge China’s grip on critical materials

## Summary

Scimplify has raised $25 million while its annualized revenue run rate has tripled to $200 million. The company is now eyeing a U.S. expansion to challenge China's grip on critical materials using artificial intelligence.

## Why It Matters

The intersection of AI tooling, supply chain logistics, and heavy industry material sourcing opens up new domains for software automation and data-driven infrastructure.
