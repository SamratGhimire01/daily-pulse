# Tech Update

## Headline

Apple's next-generation iPhones are likely to feature higher prices due to soaring memory costs

## Summary

When Apple debuts its next generation of iPhones, they are expected to come with a higher price tag driven by soaring memory costs. This potential price hike from a supply-chain powerhouse highlights an unavoidable memory crunch with no end in sight, a trend being called "chipflation."

## Why It Matters

Rising hardware costs and supply chain memory crunches directly impact development budgets, hardware testing environments, and the pricing strategies of consumer devices we build for.
