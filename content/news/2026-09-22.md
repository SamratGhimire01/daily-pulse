The UK Government Faces a Decision Over Palantir’s NHS Contract

## Summary

The UK government, facing its relationship with Big Tech and Washington, must decide whether to cut Palantir's £330 million NHS contract. The situation serves as a major test for the prime minister, particularly given past resistance from figures like Andy Burnham.

## Why It Matters

Public sector contracts involving major data and tech vendors highlight the ongoing geopolitical, regulatory, and ethical tensions surrounding government IT infrastructure.
