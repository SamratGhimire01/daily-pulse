# Tech Update

## Headline

Instagram Addresses Fake AI-Influencer Accounts and Renames Label

## Summary

Instagram is taking steps to address the rise of difficult-to-spot fake AI-influencer accounts pretending to be human. Additionally, the platform is renaming the "AI creator" label to "AI-generated profile" to clearly indicate when a profile features an AI-generated person instead of a real human being, responding to user feedback that people dislike seeing such profiles.

## Why It Matters

Developers working with media platforms or building AI applications need to stay aware of shifting social media policies regarding synthetic identities and mandatory disclosure labels.
