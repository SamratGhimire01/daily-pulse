# Tech Update

## Headline

Michael Polansky's Startup Trains AI on Living Human Skin Tissue

## Summary

Michael Polansky has launched a startup that utilizes an AI model trained on living human skin tissue, which is kept alive for weeks outside the body, to discover new skincare compounds. Polansky, a former top deputy to Sean Parker, spent years building the company before going public.

## Why It Matters

This represents an intersection of AI and biotechnology, showcasing how machine learning models are being applied to biological wetware and novel tissue preservation techniques for compound discovery.

## Source

TechCrunch: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/michael-polansky-is-training-an-ai-model-on-skin-thats-still-alive/
