# Tech Update

## Headline

China’s Manus Raises Over $500M in First Funding Round Since Split With Meta

## Summary

Manus has raised more than $500 million in its first funding round since splitting with Meta. The round was led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, with participation from existing shareholders including Tencent, HSG (formerly Sequoia China), and ZhenFund.

## Why It Matters

Major funding rounds in the AI and tech space often signal shifting capital availability and market focus for companies scaling large platforms. Keeping track of these investments helps developers understand where significant industry resources are being concentrated.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Amazon Launches New Line of Alexa Tablets to Rival the iPad

## Summary

Amazon is launching a new lineup of tablets called Alexa Tablets, abandoning the Fire name used for the past 15 years. These new devices are designed to bring Alexa Plus to users and aim to compete more directly in the broader tablet market.

## Why It Matters

Changes in Amazon's hardware strategy and branding introduce new user interfaces and ecosystem capabilities that developers must account for when building software optimized for Alexa and new tablet form factors.
