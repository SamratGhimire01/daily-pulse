# Tech Update

## Headline

Northeastern University Study Tests Television ACR Tracking and Opt-Out Effectiveness

## Summary

While it is well known that TVs gather information, researchers at Northeastern University monitored 12 TVs from 10 manufacturers to figure out what and how they track, and whether opting out of automatic content recognition (ACR) actually works. The study investigates the difficulty consumers face in stopping this data collection.

## Why It matters

For developers working with smart devices, telemetry, or privacy compliance, understanding user tracking mechanisms and the efficacy of opt-outs is critical for building trustworthy systems.
