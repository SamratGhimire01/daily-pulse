## Headline

Tasks.txt: A Free macOS Productivity App That Is Just a Text Document

## Summary

The totally free macOS app Tasks.txt functions as a simple text document rather than a traditional, feature-rich task manager. It compensates for its lack of advanced features with sheer elegance.

## Why It Matters

For developers who prefer minimalist, distraction-free workflows rooted in plain text over bloated productivity software, this app offers a refreshing approach.
