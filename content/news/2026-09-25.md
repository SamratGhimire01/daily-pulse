## Headline

US Government Files Application to Support X's Legal Challenge Against EU Fine

## Summary

The Trump administration has filed an application to help Elon Musk and X avoid a $137 million fine issued by the European Union under the Digital Services Act. Donald Trump has referred to the EU's enforcement action as "overseas extortion."

## Why It Matters

This case highlights how geopolitical tensions and state-level diplomacy increasingly intersect with major regulatory enforcement of tech platforms under frameworks like the Digital Services Act.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Phishing Scams Disguised as Party Invitations Target User Data

## Summary

Email phishing scams designed to look like digital invitations from services such as Evite or Paperless Post are being used to steal personal data. Interestingly, these deceptive messages have inadvertently prompted some recipients to reach out and reconnect with old friends or flames.

## Why It matters

Security-minded professionals must remain vigilant against evolving social engineering tactics that mimic legitimate everyday communication tools like event invitations to bypass user skepticism.

## Source

WIRED: https://www.wired.com/story/party-invite-phishing-scams-are-the-new-missed-connections/
