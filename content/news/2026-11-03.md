# Tech Update

## Headline

Roli Collapses into Insolvency, Leaving Customers with Unsupported Gear

## Summary

The inventive musical instrument maker Roli has collapsed into insolvency. This leaves buyers empty-handed and current users scrambling to keep their specialized hardware and gear functioning.

## Why It Matters

It serves as a stark reminder of the hardware-software dependency risk in tech ecosystems, where the sudden failure of a vendor can instantly brick or strand custom user hardware.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Bambu Announces Nine-Nozzle H3C Color-Mixing Toolchanger 3D Printer

## Summary

Bambu has announced the H3C, a nine-nozzle 3D printer that represents the next evolution in 3D printing. It can swap materials on the fly, mix different colors for a near-rainbow output, and combine soft and hard materials.

## Why It Matters

Advanced multi-material and multi-color rapid prototyping capabilities directly accelerate how quickly physical enclosures, custom peripherals, and mechanical parts can be iterated on in-house.
