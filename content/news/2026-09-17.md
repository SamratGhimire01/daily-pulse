# Tech Update

## Headline

Louis Rossmann Grassroots Tour Highlights Device Ownership Resistance

## Summary

A grassroots live tour by YouTuber Louis Rossmann focuses on growing user frustration with companies restricting device control. The movement highlights people wanting to own and manage their own hardware without corporate interference.

## Why It Matters

This resistance directly impacts the developer and tech community's ability to tinker with, repair, and maintain the hardware and software they rely on.
