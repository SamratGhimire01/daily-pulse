## Headline

California's Proposition 45 could hide a loophole for data centers

## Summary

Environmental advocates warn that California's Proposition 45—pitched as a fast track for essential infrastructure—could give data center developers a loophole to evade public scrutiny. The ballot measure would shorten timelines for state-mandated environmental assessments and make it harder for residents to challenge eligible projects in court.

## Why It Matters

Developers and tech infrastructure planners need to monitor state policies like Prop 45, as changes to environmental assessments and legal challenges directly impact data center deployment timelines.
