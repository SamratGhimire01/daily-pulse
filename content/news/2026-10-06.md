# Tech Update

## Headline

Tomorrow Doesn't Matter's Neo Headphones That Roll Up Into a Bluetooth Speaker Are Available in the US

## Summary

Following their CES 2026 debut and a successful Kickstarter campaign last February, TDM's Neo headphones are now available in the US market. The device features a flexible headband and extra drivers that allow the headphones to roll up into a Bluetooth speaker.

## Why It Matters

This hardware design showcases innovative mechanical engineering and form-factor flexibility for portable audio devices. It highlights how unusual hardware designs can move from crowdfunding to actual retail availability.

Otherlab's Lightfoot Solar-Powered Urban Scooter Designed to Go Farther

## Summary

Otherlab has introduced Lightfoot, a box-on-wheels urban scooter designed to travel greater distances using the power of the sun. The solar-powered vehicle is built to catch attention while extending its range outdoors.

## Why It matters

Integrating alternative power sources like solar energy into small personal vehicles presents interesting hardware-software integration and power-management challenges. It reflects ongoing innovation in micro-mobility tech hardware design.
