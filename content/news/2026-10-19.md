# Tech Update

## Headline

Apple updates guidelines to ban nudify apps masquerading as regular apps to pass review

## Summary

Apple announced that it will ban AI apps that fail to moderate offensive content. This includes updating guidelines specifically to target nudify apps that try to masquerade as regular apps to pass the app review process.

## Why It Matters

Developers working on AI-powered applications must ensure robust content moderation is in place to avoid app store rejections or bans.

## Headline

Musk pledges to give every kid in America one of his SpaceX shares

## Summary

Elon Musk has pledged to give every child in America one of his SpaceX shares. This commitment amounts to over $10 billion in shares, representing about 1% of his current net worth.

## Why It Matters

This major distribution involves a significant percentage of SpaceX equity and billions in value tied to a prominent aerospace company.
