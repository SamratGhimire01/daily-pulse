WIRED Article Argues That the Video Game Industry Should Scale Down AAA Titles

## Summary

The article discusses how the pursuit of AAA glory has led to crushing layoffs, ballooning budgets, and bland end products in the video game industry. It suggests that game makers need to stop creating massive games on the scale of 'Grand Theft Auto VI'.

## Why It matters

Engineers and developers working in gaming or resource-constrained software environments should consider the financial, operational, and structural risks of scaling projects too large.
