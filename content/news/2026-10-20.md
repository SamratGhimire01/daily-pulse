## Headline

Yelp integrates new AI features to call restaurants and manage preferences

## Summary

Yelp is embedding various new AI features into its mobile app. These capabilities include making phone calls on a user's behalf to check restaurant wait times and generating personalized recommendations that remember user preferences.

## Why It Matters

This release demonstrates practical consumer-facing AI implementations, particularly automated voice-calling agents integrated into everyday applications. Developers working on conversational AI or context-aware recommendation systems can look to this as an example of feature bundling.

# Tech Update

## Headline

Otter.ai raises $320 million from General Catalyst for meeting tool expansion

## Summary

Meeting note-taking tool Otter.ai has raised $320 million from General Catalyst’s growth fund, specifically utilizing the Customer Value Fund (CVF) which provides capital without diluting equity in exchange for returns on marketing spend. The company plans to allocate the funds toward product innovation, enterprise growth, international expansion, and its go-to-market strategy.

## Why It Matters

This funding structure offers an alternative financing model for late-stage startups beyond traditional equity dilution. Furthermore, continued heavy investment in AI meeting assistants signals strong enterprise demand for automated productivity tools.
