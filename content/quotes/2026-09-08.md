# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Heider

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank file or tackle an unfamiliar architecture, that exact friction is where the actual growth happens. It is easy to stay comfortable writing the same code we mastered months ago, but progress only lives right on the edge of our current incompetence. Leaning into that initial confusion is usually the best indicator that I am finally building something worthwhile.
