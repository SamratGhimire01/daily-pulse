# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that all grand achievements are eventually realized."

— Steven Pressfield

## Reflection

We often treat mistakes as friction, proof that we aren't moving fast enough or that our initial plan was flawed. But debugging a broken system or rewriting a clumsy function is where the actual understanding takes root. Mastery isn't about avoiding the wall, but learning how to build something better from the pieces after you hit it.
