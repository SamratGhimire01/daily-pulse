# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to smooth out the rough edges that we end up sanding away the entire shape of what we were trying to make."

— An original thought

## Reflection

Perfectionism is often just a disguised form of procrastination that prevents us from shipping meaningful work. When we spend all our time polishing the trivial details, we risk losing the core utility and character of the project. Sometimes you just have to accept the rough edges and let the creation exist in the real world.
