# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank editor for a feature outside my comfort zone, that initial friction feels like hitting a wall. But progress usually hides right behind the uncertainty of a documentation page I don't fully understand yet. Growth doesn't happen when we stick to the stack we've mastered; it happens in the messy middle of figuring it out.
