# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I stare at a blank editor and feel completely out of my depth, I try to remember that this uncomfortable friction is just the actual mechanism of learning. The moments where I feel like I'm guessing my way through a problem are usually the ones that shape me the most as a builder. Confidence doesn't come from avoiding the unknown, but from surviving it a few times.
