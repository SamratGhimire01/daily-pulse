# Daily Quote

> "Programs must be written for people to read, and only incidentally for machines to execute."

— Harold Abelson and Gerald Jay Sussman

## Reflection

It is easy to write code solely to satisfy the compiler while forgetting about the person who will have to maintain it six months later. Clean naming, thoughtful structure, and obvious logic save far more time over a project's lifespan than hyper-optimized cleverness. When building software, empathy for the next reader is just as vital as technical correctness.
