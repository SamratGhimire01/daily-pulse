# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I stare at a codebase I don't fully understand or a feature that feels just slightly out of reach, I try to remind myself that friction is the actual mechanism of learning. Comfort zones are quiet, but they are also entirely devoid of progress. Stepping into the unknown is the only way to expand what is possible.
