# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experience and actually building things that you learn how things work under the hood."

— An original thought

## Reflection

Reading documentation and watching tutorials can only take you so far before theory hits the wall of reality. There is a specific kind of clarity that only appears when your hands are on the keyboard, debugging a strange error at midnight. Building forces you to confront the gaps in your understanding that you didn't even know were there.
