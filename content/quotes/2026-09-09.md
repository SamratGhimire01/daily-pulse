# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Maher

## Reflection

Every time I open an editor to tackle a problem I've never seen before, there is a quiet moment of resistance. Yet growth only happens in that exact space of uncertainty where the documentation is sparse and the solution isn't obvious. Leaking into the unknown is the only way the map gets bigger.
