# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that we learn how to persist."

— David Bayles and Ted Orland

## Reflection

We often treat mistakes as proof that we aren't cut out for a task, but they are actually just the raw data we need to build better mental models. Every bug encountered and every broken build is simply part of the feedback loop that moves us from guessing to truly understanding. Persistence isn't about avoiding failure, but about getting curious when things inevitably fall apart.
