# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get the intuition for how things work."

— Bret Victor

## Reflection

Reading documentation and watching tutorials can only take you so far, but actually writing the code bridges the gap between theory and understanding. When you run into a broken state, the friction of debugging forces your brain to build a deeper mental model of the system. Getting your hands dirty is the fastest way to turn abstract concepts into genuine intuition.
