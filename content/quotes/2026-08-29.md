# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead, not by assuming stuff that we needn't assume."

— Alan Turing

## Reflection

We often get stuck in endless loops of planning, trying to anticipate every edge case before writing a single line of code. But the truth is that reality usually bites back in ways we could never simulate in our heads. Building something small and seeing it break gives you more insight in five minutes than five days of pure speculation ever could.
