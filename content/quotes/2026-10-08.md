# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that all great things are achieved."

— Stephen King

## Reflection

We often treat bugs and broken builds as signs that we are on the wrong path, but they are usually just the raw material of progress. Every time a system fails or a piece of logic refuses to click, it reveals the exact boundaries of our current understanding. Embracing this friction makes the eventual breakthrough feel earned rather than accidental.
