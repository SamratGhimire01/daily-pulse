# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to smooth out the roughness of our ideas that we come up with new ones."

— Richard Hamming

## Reflection

We often fall into the trap of thinking our initial ideas need to be fully polished before we start building them. In reality, the act of forcing rough concepts into actual code or design is where the real breakthroughs happen. Embracing the friction of execution is usually what turns a mediocre thought into something genuinely useful.
