# Daily Quote

> "Simplicity is prerequisite for reliability."

— Edsger W. Dijkstra

## Reflection

When I try to build things that do too much at once, the system almost always collapses under its own weight. Stripping a feature down to its absolute core isn't about cutting corners; it is about finding the clearest path to making something that actually works. True progress often looks like removing complexity rather than adding it.
