# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I hit a wall with a complex bug or an unfamiliar framework, it is easy to forget that the friction itself is the point. Growth does not happen in the comfort zone of code we have already written a dozen times. Leaning into the unknown is the only way to actually expand our capabilities as builders.
