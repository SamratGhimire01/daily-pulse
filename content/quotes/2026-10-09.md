# Daily Quote

> "It is not that I'm so smart, but I stay with the problems much longer."

— Albert Einstein

## Reflection

We often glorify raw intelligence in software development, but day-to-day progress is usually just stubbornness disguised as problem-solving. Breakthroughs rarely come from a sudden flash of genius; they come from sitting with a broken piece of code long enough to finally understand it. Persistence turns out to be the most reliable skill you can practice.
