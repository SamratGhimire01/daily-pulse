# Daily Quote

> "It is not that I'm so smart, but I stay with the questions much longer."

— Albert Einstein

## Reflection

We often celebrate the moment someone finds the answer, but the real work happens in the messy middle of not knowing. Staying curious when a bug defies logic or a new concept refuses to click is usually what separates giving up from a breakthrough. Persistence isn't just about hammering away at a keyboard; it is about having the patience to sit with confusion until it finally starts to make sense.
