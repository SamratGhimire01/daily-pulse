# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank editor for a feature I have never built before, a familiar wave of hesitation hits. Yet progress only happens right at that uncomfortable edge where competence ends and figuring it out begins. Growth as a developer isn't about knowing all the answers, but about staying curious enough to find them.
