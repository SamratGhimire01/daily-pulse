# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead, not by assuming stuff that we needn't assume."

— Alan Turing

## Reflection

We often get stuck trying to architect the perfect solution in our heads before writing a single line of code. Turing reminds us that actual progress happens when we stop guessing and start testing our assumptions against reality. The compiler or the terminal will always give us a faster, more honest answer than endless deliberation.
