# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to smooth out the rough edges that we make things worse."

— Donald Norman

## Reflection

We often want to polish a project until every friction point vanishes, but that over-refinement can strip away the actual utility or character of what we built. Sometimes the rough edges are just the natural shape of a tool doing a hard job. Instead of endlessly tweaking, it is usually better to leave the edges alone and see how it actually holds up in the wild.
