# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that we learn how to persist."

— David Bayles and Ted Orland

## Reflection

We often treat mistakes as evidence that we are off track, but they are usually the exact data we need to keep going. Every bug and broken build is just part of the feedback loop of figuring things out. Persistence isn't about avoiding failure; it's about getting better at sitting with it and learning what it has to teach.
