# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank editor for a feature I haven't built before, that familiar knot of hesitation shows up. Progress rarely happens in the comfortable zone of executing what already makes sense to us. Growth is hidden right behind the clumsy first drafts and the errors we have to patiently debug.
