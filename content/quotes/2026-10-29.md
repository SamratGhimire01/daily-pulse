# Daily Quote

> "It is not that I'm so smart, but I stay with the questions much longer."

— Albert Einstein

## Reflection

We often celebrate the flash of genius, but real progress usually comes from stubborn curiosity. Sticking with a difficult bug or a confusing architecture for days on end requires more grit than raw talent. Mastery isn't about knowing all the answers immediately, but having the patience to live in the confusion until the solution emerges.
