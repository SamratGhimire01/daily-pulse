# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank editor for a feature I have never built before, that slight spike of anxiety is actually the exact mechanism of growth. We tend to wait until we feel fully prepared before starting, but competence is almost always a retroactive reward for persistence. The only reliable way past the confusion of the unknown is to step directly into it and figure it out as you go.
