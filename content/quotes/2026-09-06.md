# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Horner

## Reflection

Every time I open an editor to build something slightly beyond my current skill level, that initial friction feels intimidating. Yet, that exact discomfort is the clearest signal that real learning is happening. Growth as a developer isn't about knowing all the answers from the start, but staying curious enough to figure them out along the way.
