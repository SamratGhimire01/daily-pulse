# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Stepping into unfamiliar territory is the only reliable way to grow as a builder. When a bug or a new architecture feels completely out of reach, it is usually just a sign that the real learning is about to begin. Confusion isn't a dead end; it's the exact threshold where progress happens.
