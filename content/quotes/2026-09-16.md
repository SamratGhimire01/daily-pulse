# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Hopper

## Reflection

Stepping into unfamiliar territory is the only reliable way to push past current limitations. Whenever a new feature or architecture feels slightly out of reach, it is usually a sign that growth is actually happening. Embracing that initial friction turns frustrating roadblocks into the very lessons that make future building easier.
