# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time we stare at a blank editor or a complex architecture diagram, we are willingly stepping into the unknown. Growth rarely happens inside our comfort zones; it lives in the messy space between a failing build and a working solution. Embracing that initial confusion is the only real way forward.
