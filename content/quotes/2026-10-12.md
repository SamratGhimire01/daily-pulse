# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical application that we truly begin to understand the constraints and possibilities of what we are building."

— An original thought

## Reflection

Reading about a system is never the same as sitting down and watching your own code fail against it. Theory gives us a roadmap, but the friction of implementation is what forces us to actually learn how the pieces fit together. Every unexpected bug is just reality pushing back and showing us the gaps in our mental model.
