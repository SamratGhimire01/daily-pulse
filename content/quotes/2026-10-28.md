# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead; we must try things out."

— J.J. Thomson

## Reflection

Theory is comfortable because it keeps us safe from being wrong, but actual progress only happens when we build something and see how it breaks. Every messy prototype and failed test teaches us constraints that no textbook can capture. Growth as a developer isn't about getting it right on the first try, but about closing the loop between our ideas and reality as quickly as possible.
