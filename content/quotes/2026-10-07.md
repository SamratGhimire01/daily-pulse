# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead; not by reinventing the wheel, but by seeing how the wheel can be turned faster, smoother, and with less friction."

— An original thought

## Reflection

We often get bogged down trying to build everything from scratch, believing that novelty equals progress. True momentum usually comes from taking existing primitives and refining how they interact. Mastery isn't about ignoring what works, but understanding it deeply enough to make it effortless.
