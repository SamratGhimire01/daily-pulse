# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to smooth out the rough edges that we end up sanding away the core."

— An original thought

## Reflection

In our pursuit of clean architecture and perfect abstractions, it is easy to over-engineer until the original purpose gets lost in the process. Sometimes the friction we are trying so hard to eliminate is actually what gives the system its shape and utility. We have to remind ourselves that working software doesn't need to be immaculate to be valuable.
