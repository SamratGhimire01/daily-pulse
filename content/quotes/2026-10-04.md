# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead; not by reinventing the wheel, but by seeing how the wheel can be turned faster and better."

— An original thought

## Reflection

Building software often tempts us to create everything from scratch in the name of purity. Real progress usually happens, however, when we take existing foundations and carefully adapt them to solve new problems. Moving forward is less about solitary invention and more about thoughtful iteration.
