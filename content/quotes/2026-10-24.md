# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that all great things are achieved."

— Stephen King

## Reflection

We often treat bugs and broken builds as evidence that we are off track, but they are usually just the material we have to work through. Every time a system fails or a piece of logic breaks, it gives us a clearer picture of how the underlying problem actually behaves. Progress isn't about avoiding mistakes; it is about how closely we pay attention to them when they happen.
