# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that all learning takes place."

— Ray Bradbury

## Reflection

We often treat bugs and broken builds as interruptions to our actual work, but they are usually where the real understanding happens. Bashing your head against a difficult problem forces you to build mental models that surface-level tutorials can never teach you. Mastery isn't about avoiding mistakes; it is about learning how to decode them when they inevitably appear.
