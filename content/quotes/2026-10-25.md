# Daily Quote

> "It is not that I'm so smart, but I stay with the questions much longer."

— Albert Einstein

## Reflection

We often celebrate the moment someone finds the answer, as if breakthroughs happen in a sudden flash of brilliance. But the real work usually happens in the uncomfortable middle, where you simply refuse to put a hard problem down. Staying curious and resisting the urge to settle for a surface-level fix is what turns a frustrating bug into a deep understanding.
