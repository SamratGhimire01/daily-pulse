# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead; not by reinventing the wheel, but by thoroughly understanding how the old one works before trying to improve it."

— An original thought

## Reflection

We often rush to build new things from scratch, treating existing tools as black boxes to be replaced rather than mastered. True progress usually happens when we slow down, look under the hood, and genuinely understand the mechanics of what came before us. Building better software is rarely about chasing the newest trend, but rather about developing a deeper intuition for the fundamentals.
