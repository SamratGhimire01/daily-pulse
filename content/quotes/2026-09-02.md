# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Horner

## Reflection

Stepping into unfamiliar territory is the only reliable way to grow as a developer. When a new bug or system architecture feels completely out of reach, it is usually just a sign that learning is actually happening. Instead of avoiding the unknown, treating it as the main curriculum makes the friction worthwhile.
