# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I stare at a blank editor or an unfamiliar codebase, that initial friction feels like a warning sign to stop. But stepping into the unknown is actually the only reliable mechanism for growth in this field. Every meaningful tool or system I've built started as something I had no business trying to build yet.
