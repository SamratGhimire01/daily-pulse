# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I stare at a codebase I don't fully understand or a feature I've never built before, that initial friction feels like a wall. But the only way forward is to step directly into the confusion and break things until they start to make sense. Growth doesn't happen in the comfort zone of known solutions; it happens in the messy middle of figuring it out.
