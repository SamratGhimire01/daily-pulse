# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Hellen

## Reflection

Every time I open a new documentation page or stare at an unfamiliar error message, it feels a bit like standing at the edge of a cliff. Yet, growth only happens right at that uncomfortable boundary between what is familiar and what is entirely unknown. Embracing the friction of not knowing is the real job description of being a builder.
