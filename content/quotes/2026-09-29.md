# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— Francis Hellen

## Reflection

Stepping into unfamiliar territory is the only reliable way to push past current limits. When a codebase or a concept feels completely opaque, the confusion is usually a sign of impending growth rather than a lack of capability. Embracing that initial friction makes the eventual breakthrough possible.
