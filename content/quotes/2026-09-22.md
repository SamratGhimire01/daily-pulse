# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank editor or stare down a strange bug, I am reminded that growth only happens in the uncomfortable space between what I know and what I need to figure out. Mastery isn't about avoiding the unknown; it is about developing the patience to navigate it. The best code I write always starts with a moment of feeling entirely unequipped for the task.
