# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to smooth out the rough edges that we make things worse."

— Donald Norman

## Reflection

We often want our software and workflows to feel seamless right away, but over-polishing early usually hides the actual problems we need to solve. Leaving things a bit raw gives us the friction required to see how the system actually behaves. Sometimes the best progress comes from letting the rough edges stay visible until the shape of the solution becomes obvious.
