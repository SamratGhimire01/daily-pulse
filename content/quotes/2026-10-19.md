# Daily Quote

> "It's through practicing the art of failure that we learn how to make things work."

— Ted Nelson

## Reflection

We tend to treat bugs and broken builds as interruptions to our work, but they are actually the primary mechanism of progress. Every time something fails, it gives us a precise boundary of what doesn't work, narrowing the path toward the solution. Learning to sit comfortably with that friction is what separates frustration from actual problem solving.
