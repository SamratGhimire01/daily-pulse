# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I hit a wall with a complex bug or an unfamiliar framework, I try to remind myself that the confusion is the actual work. Growth rarely happens inside the boundaries of what we already know how to build. You have to be willing to stumble through the unknown to make any real progress.
