# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to smooth out the rough edges that we end up sanding away the entire shape of what we were trying to make."

— An original thought

## Reflection

Perfectionism is often just a sneaky form of procrastination disguised as care. When we spend all our time tweaking minor details, we risk losing the core utility and spirit of what we set out to build. Sometimes shipping a rough, honest version matters far more than endless polishing.
