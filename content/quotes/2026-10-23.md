# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Every time I open a blank editor for a feature I have never built before, that familiar anxiety creeps in. Yet growth only happens right at the edge of competence, where the documentation is sparse and the solution requires trial and error. Embracing that initial confusion is the only real way forward.
