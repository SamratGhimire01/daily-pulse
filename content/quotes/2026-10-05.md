# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I stare down a new framework or an unfamiliar codebase, that initial feeling of total incompetence is hard to shake. But progress rarely happens inside our comfort zones; it lives in the messy space where we have to figure things out as we go. Every bug that baffles us today is just the scaffolding for a better understanding tomorrow.
