# Daily Quote

> "It's through trying to do things we don't know how to do that we learn how to do them."

— William James

## Reflection

Whenever I stare at a blank editor or a complex architecture problem I've never touched before, I try to remember that the friction is the actual work. Growth rarely happens inside our comfort zones of familiar syntax and solved problems. Pushing forward into the unknown is the only way the unfamiliar eventually becomes second nature.
