# Daily Quote

> "It's through practical experiment that we get ahead; not by reinventing the wheel, but by thoroughly understanding how the old one turned."

— An original thought

## Reflection

We often rush to build the next new thing without fully appreciating the mechanics of what came before. True progress usually comes from taking the time to deeply understand existing systems rather than just skimming their surface. When we respect the fundamentals, our own creations become that much more resilient.
